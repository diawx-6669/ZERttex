3. Volume assumption

Goal: 30% foreign travelers within 24 months Case fact. Today ≈ 136 foreign travelers (4% of 3,400). Keeping ≈ 3,264 domestic travelers, 30% foreign means ≈ 4,663 total and ≈ 1,399 foreign, so ≈ 1,263 additional foreign travelers per year by month 24. Launch is in month 4; volume ramps linearly from launch to the full run rate in month 24 (month m gets (m − 3) ÷ 21 of it), then stays flat.

4. Payback model

Formula. Monthly net = additional travelers × 13,200 − costs. Costs: 12.0M one-time spread over months 1-3; the other 8.0M over months 1-12; 300,000 per month from month 13. Payback = first month in which cumulative cash is ≥ 0. Downside scenario: 30% fewer travelers than planned (× 0.7), everything else unchanged.

Reading it honestly. The base case pays back within 48 months for any margin from 20% up. The downside needs a margin of about 30% or more, so the margin is the first number to check with the company's real costs. If the pilot shows weaker demand, the levers are: shift marketing to referrals and organic Xiaohongshu content, delay paid ads, and keep running costs at the 300,000 KZT per month floor. The model is also live and adjustable on the Impact page of the prototype.
