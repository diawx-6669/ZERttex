Financial calculations and business model

Saryarqa Go. All amounts in KZT.

1. Business model

The traveler pays Saryarqa Routes for the tour, the same way local customers do now. Saryarqa Go itself is not sold to anyone. It is the new sales channel that lets foreign travelers find, understand and pay for the tours.

In our calculation we count only one source of income: the margin on additional foreign travelers. The average price is 44,000 KZT (case data). We assume that 30% of it is left after paying guides, transport, accommodation and payment fees, so 13,200 KZT per traveler (our assumption). We do not count three other possible sources, so the result is on the careful side:

  • optional extras sold on the tour page, like horse riding, boats or a single room;
  • longer trips, because the trip builder makes it easy to combine two or three tours;
  • new travelers who come through postcard links, which cost almost nothing to get.

Note: 150,000,000 / 3,400 = 44,118 KZT, which is a little higher than the 44,000 KZT average price given in the case. We use 44,000 KZT.

3. How many travelers we expect

To reach 30% foreign travelers the company needs about 1,263 additional foreign travelers per year (see section 3 of the main report). We launch in month 4 and expect the number to grow step by step until it reaches the full level in month 24 (in month m we get (m - 3) / 21 of the full level). After that we keep it flat, which is also a careful choice.

4. Payback

Each month we calculate: additional travelers x 13,200 KZT, minus costs. The 5.1M one-time costs are spread over months 1-3, the other 2.9M over months 1-12, and from month 13 the cost is 250,000 KZT per month. The payback month is the first month when the total since the start is no longer negative. For the pessimistic (downside) scenario we use 30% fewer travelers and keep everything else the same.

What if our margin is wrong?

The 30% margin is our biggest assumption, so we checked how the payback changes:

Margin20%25%30%35%40%
Payback, base casemonth 26month 23month 20month 19month 17
Payback, downsidemonth 34month 28month 25month 23month 21

In the base case the project pays back in month 26 even with a 20% margin. In the downside case it pays back in month 34 with a 20% margin and in month 25 with 30%. So the first thing we would check with the company is its real cost per traveler. If the pilot shows lower demand, we would move more of the marketing to free channels (postcards and our own Xiaohongshu posts), delay paid ads, and keep running costs at 250,000 KZT per month.
