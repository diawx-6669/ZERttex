Saryarqa Go: book Kazakhstan with confidence

An English / Chinese booking app that helps Saryarqa Routes reach 30% foreign travelers within 24 months. Team: ____________________

1. Summary

Saryarqa Routes sells guided trips almost only to Kazakhstan residents because its sales process (Instagram messages, WhatsApp, phone, Kaspi or cash, a Russian-only one-page site) does not work for foreigners Case fact. Saryarqa Go is a bilingual booking app built as one closed loop (Discover, Plan, Verify, Book, Travel, Share) that answers the four complaints foreign guests actually made. We start with Chinese-speaking independent travelers. The 12-week MVP fits the 20,000,000 KZT year-1 budget and, under stated assumptions, pays back in month 32 (base) or month 44 (downside).

2. Problem and relevance

Saryarqa Routes (Astana, founded 2020; 9 staff, ~25 freelance guides) served 3,400 travelers in 2025 with revenue of 150,000,000 KZT and an average price of 44,000 KZT; about 96% were from Kazakhstan. The goal is 30% foreign travelers within 24 months Case fact. Foreign guests named four problems, and each one stops a sale before payment:

Guest said Case factWhere the sale breaksRoot cause today
"It is unclear how to book."Before first contactChat-only sales; Russian-only one-page website
"I am not sure the company is trustworthy."Before paying abroadNo visible proof; no way to meet the people
"My foreign card does not work."At paymentKaspi transfer or cash only
"I do not know what is included."Before committingNo structured description of the price

Why Chinese-speaking independent travelers first

  • Fast-growing market. Kazakhstan received about 655,000 visitors from China in 2024, +78% vs 367,000 in 2023, according to the Ministry of Tourism and Sports [1][2] Verified source. China was the largest source market outside the near abroad in 2024 [3] Verified source.
  • No visa barrier. A mutual visa-free regime has applied since November 2023: up to 30 days per visit, max 90 days in 180 [1] Verified source. The decision is about trust, clarity and payment, exactly the four complaints.
  • Big inbound flow overall. Kazakhstan counted 15.3 million foreign visitors for tourism purposes in 2024 [3] and 15.7 million in the first nine months of 2025 [5] Verified source.
  • Product fit. Nature and photography (Charyn, Kolsai, Burabay) and Silk Road culture (Turkestan) match what independent travelers look for Proposal.

Method note: an analyst estimate of 241,700 Chinese visitors in 2025 [4] uses a different method from the Ministry figure for 2024 [1], so the two are not compared. National visitor counts [3][5] and World Bank / UN Tourism overnight arrivals [6] use different definitions.

The size of the job

About 4% of 3,400 ≈ 136 travelers are foreign today. Keeping ≈ 3,264 domestic travelers, 30% foreign needs ≈ 4,663 travelers in total, so ≈ 1,399 foreign: about 1,263 more foreign travelers per year, ≈ 105 per month. One complaint fixed at a time will not get there; the whole buying journey must work for a foreigner.

3. The product: Saryarqa Go

One closed loop instead of a pile of features. Every step removes one doubt, and the last step brings the next traveler back to the first.

StepWhat the traveler getsComplaint answered
DiscoverTour pages in EN / 中文; "What's included" in four groups: Included, Optional extra, Not included, Not yet confirmed"I do not know what is included"
PlanTrip builder: arrival, real tour days, Almaty / Turkestan / Astana transfers, city days, cost estimate"It is unclear how to book"
VerifyTrust Center: guide intro video, 5-minute video call before paying, honest verification checklist, refund rules; bilingual concierge that hands off to a human when unsure"I am not sure the company is trustworthy"
BookThree steps (trip, lead traveler, payment); international card via the provider's hosted checkout; Alipay / WeChat Pay after provider approval; price in KZT with USD / CNY"My foreign card does not work"; "unclear how to book"
TravelTimeline with daily check-ins, opt-in family updates (no GPS), weather and road-closure rescheduling, audio guide in ChineseTrust during the trip
SharePostcard sized for Xiaohongshu / WeChat Moments with a referral link that opens step 1 for a friendLow-cost acquisition

4. Originality and unique value

  • "Not yet confirmed" as a feature. Park fees, guesthouse names, the Kaindy 4×4 transfer and Chinese-guide availability are shown openly and confirmed in writing before the trip. Honesty about the unknown is the trust signal.
  • Meet the guide before you pay. For a traveler abroad, the guide is the company. A short video and an optional 5-minute call turn an unknown firm into a person.
  • A concierge that refuses to guess. It answers in the traveler's language from verified tour data only and passes availability, discount and edge-case questions to a manager.
  • Safety for the family at home. Opt-in, one trip only, ends automatically, shares check-in status rather than live location.
  • The postcard closes the loop. Content the traveler wants to post anyway becomes the cheapest acquisition channel.
  • Honesty labels everywhere. Every claim in the app is marked Case fact, Verified source, Proposal, Demo data or Needs confirmation.

Rule: anything that needs a third party's approval or live data waits for later (live LLM concierge, Alipay / WeChat Pay, live weather, GPS sharing, offline audio, Trip.com / Klook). The MVP therefore ships in 12 weeks regardless. Tools: React + TypeScript web app (reuses the prototype), managed database with admin panel, payment provider hosted checkout with webhooks. People: a contracted designer, two developers and a Chinese localizer, with one Saryarqa Routes staff member as product owner Proposal.

Proof: a working clickable prototype already covers the whole loop (booking with validation and a simulated declined payment, bilingual concierge, trip builder, trust center, in-trip screen, postcard, live payback calculator). An automated test checks 75 acceptance points, including phone width and dark mode; all pass. Payments are simulated in the prototype (TEST MODE); no card or passport data is collected.

Revenue comes from the margin on additional foreign travelers (44,000 KZT × 30% assumed margin) Proposal. Break-even needs 1,516 extra foreign travelers in total. Payback falls inside 48 months for any margin of 20% or more in the base case; the downside needs about 30% or more. Full budget, revenue model, yearly cash flow and sensitivity are in the separate financial document.

Impact Proposal: a second, foreign revenue stream for the company beside its domestic base; travelers get clear prices, their own payment method and a guide they have met; Kazakhstan gets an easy-to-book, trustworthy experience that Chinese travelers share on their own social platforms, spreading demand to regions beyond the big cities.
