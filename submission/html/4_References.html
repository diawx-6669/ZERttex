Sources of statistics and facts

Accessed 25 October 2026. The team opens every link again before submission. Numbers in [brackets] are used in the main report.

  1. [1] The Times of Central Asia (19 June 2025). Article on the launch of the 2025 Year of China Tourism in Kazakhstan, citing the Ministry of Tourism and Sports of the Republic of Kazakhstan. 655,000 visitors from China in 2024 (+78% vs 367,000 in 2023); mutual visa-free regime since November 2023, up to 30 days per visit, max 90 days in 180.
    https://timesca.com/kazakhstan-launches-2025-year-of-china-tourism/
  2. [2] The Astana Times (February 2025). Tourist Numbers from China to Kazakhstan Surge 78% in 2024. Corroborates the 2024 figure.
    https://astanatimes.com/2025/02/tourist-numbers-from-china-to-kazakhstan-surge-78-in-2024/
  3. [3] The Astana Times (June 2025). Who's Visiting Kazakhstan? These Five Countries Top List, citing the Tourism Industry Committee. 15.3 million foreign visitors for tourism purposes in 2024; China first outside the near abroad with 655,000.
    https://astanatimes.com/2025/06/whos-visiting-kazakhstan-these-five-countries-top-list/
  4. [4] Orda.kz, citing Energyprom.kz analysts. Chinese Tourist Arrivals in Kazakhstan Rose Sharply in 2025, Analysts Say. 241,700 Chinese citizens in 2025 (+38.9%); Russia 429,800. Different method from [1]; not compared directly.
    https://en.orda.kz/chinese-tourist-arrivals-in-kazakhstan-rose-sharply-in-2025-analysts-say-10111/
  5. [5] The Astana Times (February 2026). Kazakhstan Reports Tourism Growth Surge as Visitor Numbers and Revenues Climb in 2025. 15.7 million foreign visitors for tourism purposes in the first nine months of 2025, excluding labour migrants and permanent residents.
    https://astanatimes.com/2026/02/kazakhstan-reports-tourism-growth-surge-as-visitor-numbers-and-revenues-climb-in-2025/
  6. [6] World Bank. International tourism, number of arrivals (ST.INT.ARVL), Kazakhstan. Data from UN Tourism (World Tourism Organization), CC BY 4.0. Counts overnight inbound visitors; not comparable with national visitor counts.
    https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/ST.INT.ARVL?locations=KZ
  7. [7] Bureau of National Statistics of the Agency for Strategic Planning and Reforms of the Republic of Kazakhstan. Tourism statistics: dynamic tables ("Number of inbound tourism visitors"; "Incoming visitors served, by nationality"). Official series for checking the figures above.
    https://stat.gov.kz/en/industries/businessstatistics/stat-tourism/dynamic-tables/
